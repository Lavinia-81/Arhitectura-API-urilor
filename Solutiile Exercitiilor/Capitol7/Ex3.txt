Ex3. Adaugă headerele standard
Cerință: RateLimit-Policy, RateLimit-Remaining, RateLimit-Reset, X-RateLimit-Plan.

-----

R => Soluție

reply.header("RateLimit-Policy", `${ratePlans[plan].rpm};w=60`);
reply.header("RateLimit-Remaining", result.remaining);
reply.header("RateLimit-Reset", 60);
reply.header("X-RateLimit-Plan", plan);
